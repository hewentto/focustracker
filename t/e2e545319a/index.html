The gate on everything else. Windred 2024 (N=60,977): sleep regularity predicted mortality better than duration, and duration added nothing once regularity was in the model.
The strongest causal mechanism in the review. Wright et al. 2013: one week of natural light advanced melatonin onset ~2 hours, within-subject, objective endpoint — and late chronotypes shifted most. Phase-resetting is daily by nature; a long Saturday walk does nothing for Tuesday.
Exercise logging is the only self-monitoring category that survives long-term: 61% sustained at 11 months vs 21% for diet logging. Missed the run? 3 × 30 s all-out stair climbs counts — that protocol produced +7% VO2peak from ~27 total minutes of work over 6 weeks.
Gardiner 2023 (24 studies): −45 min total sleep, −11.4 min deep sleep. Feeds straight back into the anchor.
WEIS 2017 (N=455): imposed blocking +22–24% productivity; the identical tool under user control produced nothing, with 30% never switching it on. If you can dismiss it in one tap it's a dashboard.
Harkin 2016 (138 RCTs, N=19,951): progress monitoring → goal attainment d+ = 0.40, and recorded beats unrecorded. Monitoring behaviour improves behaviour (0.79); monitoring outcomes improves outcomes (0.62). They don't cross over — which is why this counts behaviours, not tickets.

        Storage & syncnot configured

        Saves automatically in this browser. To share across devices, paste a GitHub token once per device — each sync writes focus-tracker-data.json and focus-log.csv into a secret Gist on your account. Nothing is written to the public repo.

        Import from FitNotes

        FitNotes already holds every set you've lifted, so there is no reason to retype loads here. Import its CSV and the lift notepad fills itself, the set-per-muscle counts become real instead of estimated, and lift days get ticked automatically.

        Getting the file out of FitNotes

        In FitNotes: overflow menu → Settings → Data → Spreadsheet Export → Save Export (or Share Export to send it straight to Drive, Dropbox or email). Pick Workout data.

        Not the Backup. The automatic Google Drive backup writes a .fitnotes database into Drive's hidden application data folder, which is scoped so only FitNotes itself can read it — you can see how much space it uses and disconnect it, but nothing, including this app, can open it. It exists to restore FitNotes onto a new phone. The Spreadsheet Export is the separate feature, and it is an ordinary CSV you can see.

        Nothing is overwritten blindly: a day that already records a run keeps it and gains the lift as its second session, and your ticks, notes and times are never touched. Both the Android (Weight + Weight Unit) and iOS (Weight (kg)) column layouts are accepted, and pounds are converted.
